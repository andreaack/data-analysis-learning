### Data Analyst

> They turn the numbers into business insights

****Responsibilities****:
- Collect & Clean structured data sets
- Analyze trends & performance metrics
- Create Dashboards, Reports & Visualizations to communicate insights to stakeholders.

### Data Engineer

> Building the foundation of Data Systems

****Responsibilities****:
- Build data pipelines and databases
- Develop ETL processes
- Ensure data reliability, quality, and scalability
-  Working with data scientists to ensure the accuracy and consistency of the data used for analysis
- Staying up-to-date with the latest data storage technologies and best practices

### Data Science

> Predicting the future with Machine Learning & Analytics

****Responsibilities****:
- Build ML models and algorithms
- Run experiments and optimize predictions
- Share insights with technical and business teams
- Collecting & Cleaning large data sets
- -Communicating insights and recommendations to stakeholders
